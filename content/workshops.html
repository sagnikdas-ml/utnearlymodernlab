International workshop · 26–28 November 2026

Francis Bacon’s New Atlantis: 400 years after

Three days of talks, shared readings, and discussion on Bacon’s New Atlantis, its experiments, and its visions of collective knowledge.

Where: LIAS, University of Technology Nuremberg, Ulmenstrasse 52 I, Seminar Room 2, ground floor. Hybrid participation via Zoom.

Organised by: Dana Jalobeanu, Rodolfo Garau, and Andrei Bunaciu.

With Niall Dilucia, Tobias Hirsch, Scott Mandelbrote, Doina Cristina Rusu, Cristiny Sasu, and Melania Țucureanu.
