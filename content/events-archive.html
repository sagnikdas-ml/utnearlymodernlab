Bucharest–Princeton Seminar in Early Modern Philosophy

Summer editions, 2001–2017

The seminar began in 2001 as a two-week summer school in philosophy and the history of ideas. The editions below are transcribed from the seminar’s published history. That history says the summer meetings continued through 2019; it does not give individual edition details for 2018 or 2019.

  1. 2001

    Spaces of Freedom in Modern Thought

    Individuals, Reformation Projects, Multiple Societies · Tescani · 2–13 September

  2. 2002

    Enseigner l’histoire des idées comme pratique de la liberté

    Modèles d’explication concurrents dans la pensée moderne · Macea · 30 August–13 September

  3. 2003

    Souls, Clocks, Atoms and Forces

    Competing Models of Individuals in Early Modern Thought · Macea · 30 August–14 September

  4. 2004

    Theories of Matter and Mind in the 17th Century

    Arad · 6–12 September

  5. 2005

    The Great Instauration

    Science, Philosophy and the Reformation of Knowledge in the 17th Century · Bran · 2–7 July

  6. 2006

    Disseminating Knowledge in the Seventeenth Century

    Centres and Peripheries in the Republic of Letters · Bran · 30 July–4 August

  7. 2007

    Experiments, Reason and Revelation

    The Moral Value of Studying Nature in Early Modernity · Bran · 28 July–3 August

  8. 2008

    8th edition

    Mălâncrav · 29 July–4 August

  9. 2009

    9th edition · Spinoza’s Tractatus theologico-politicus

    Bran · 25–31 July

  10. 2010

    Non-Mechanical Philosophies in the Seventeenth Century

    Bran · 30 June–6 July

  11. 2011

    Collaborative Aspects of Early Modern Thought

    Philosophical Correspondence and the Republic of Letters · Bran · 2–7 July

  12. 2012

    The Battle for “Scientia” in the Seventeenth Century

    Bran · 29 June–4 July

  13. 2013

    The Losers of the Scientific Revolution

    Bran · 5–10 July

  14. 2014

    De rerum natura

    Naturalism, Supernaturalism, Unnaturalism · Bran · 8–13 July

  15. 2015

    Philosophical Conversations

    Intellectual Interactions in the 17th Century · Bran · 12–17 July

  16. 2016

    The Architecture of Reason

    Laws, Axioms and Principles in Early Modern Thought · Alba Iulia · 12–16 July

  17. 2017

    17th edition

    Bran · 28 June–4 July · Organizers: Dana Jalobeanu, Daniel Garber, Mihnea Dobre and Oana Matei

The seminar history describes the series as interdisciplinary, bringing together philosophy, intellectual history, history and philosophy of science, and history of art.

Princeton–Bucharest Virtual Seminar

Online programme, 2020–2022

When the planned 2020 summer meeting was cancelled during the COVID-19 pandemic, the seminar moved online. The Institute for Research in the Humanities at the University of Bucharest preserves programme pages for Summer and Fall 2020, Spring and Fall 2021, and Spring and Fall 2022.
