Francis Bacon, 1626–2026: Four Centuries of Thought, New Horizons for Research

This international conference marked the four-hundredth anniversary of Francis Bacon’s death. It aimed to reassess Bacon’s philosophical, scientific, literary, and cultural legacy and the complexity of his reception across early modern Europe.

Dates
12–13 June 2026
Venue
University of Technology Nuremberg (UTN)
Organizers
Prof. Dr. Dana Jalobeanu and Dr. Rodolfo Garau

The conference brought together scholars in Bacon studies and related areas, including early modern philosophy, history of science, and literary studies.
