Princeton Bucharest Nuremberg Seminar

Error, Falsehood and Forgery in Early Modern Philosophy and the Sciences

The 25th anniversary edition of the seminar was dedicated to the category of error in the early modern period: how philosophers understood falsehood, whether error could have a positive heuristic role, and how early modern thinkers engaged with deliberate falsehood and forgery.

Dates
5–10 July 2026
Venue
Kloster Speinshart, near Nuremberg
Organizers
Dana Jalobeanu, Daniel Garber, and Rodolfo Garau

The 2026 meeting was the 25th anniversary edition of the Princeton–Bucharest seminar in Early Modern Philosophy.
