The Rome–Nuremberg–Oxford Postgraduate Summer School for Humanities Research 2026

Writing Practices: Historical and Philosophical Perspectives

This inaugural summer school was planned as a yearly forum for teaching and learning about new methods and avenues of humanities research. Its first edition focused on writing practices, from drafting and revising to moving from manuscript to publication.

The programme brought together PhD and master’s students for coursework, presentations with feedback, group discussions, collaborative writing sessions, reading groups, and evening lectures.

Dates
22–25 September 2026
Venue
